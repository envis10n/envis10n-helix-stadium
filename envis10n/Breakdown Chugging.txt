This is my rendition of a "chugging" metalcore tone. Heavy, almost grating distortion. The thunderous bass during palm muted sections.

You can adjust the overall level using your guitar's volume knob. Lower volume will bring the intensity down allowing for rhythm and lead tones easily.

I did the EQ based on my guitar with the tone knob towards 1 or 2 o'clock. Going high brings up the thrashing, while going low mellows and gives a slightly warmer feel.
